How to read this board

Implied value = TVL × multiple × growth × age × chains. The multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of token-bearing protocols in the same category (at least 3 of them, at least 0.05 — otherwise the overall median 0.214). Growth is the 7-day TVL change damped by half and clamped, so a one-week spike cannot multiply a valuation. Age adds a little for every year a protocol has been listed. Chains add 2% each, at most 4.

Records no token means the snapshot lists no token for the protocol — it is not proof one does not exist somewhere. Token already trades rows are protocols whose token is already live and trading; they stay on the board because the watchlist must be covered in full, and each one carries its evidence in its detail view. Two protocols were left out entirely, both off the watchlist, because their tokens are already live: Theo Network thUSD and Circle Gateway. Farm steps are concrete starting points — an amount, an action, a time — not promises of a launch.
